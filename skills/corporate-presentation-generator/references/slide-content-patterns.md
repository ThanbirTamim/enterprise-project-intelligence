# Slide content patterns

- **Executive summary slide**: 3-5 bullets, each citing or clearly labeled illustrative.
- **Architecture slide**: diagram (see `software-reverse-engineering` notation) + 2-3 bullets on
  what's verified vs. inferred.
- **Opportunity/roadmap slide**: table or short list referencing the opportunity matrix IDs, not
  restating full detail (link to the appendix/report instead of overflowing the slide).
- **Risk slide**: one row per risk with severity and mitigation, pulled from the risk register
  template.
- **Avoid**: paragraphs of prose on a slide; unlabeled numbers; uncredited screenshots.
